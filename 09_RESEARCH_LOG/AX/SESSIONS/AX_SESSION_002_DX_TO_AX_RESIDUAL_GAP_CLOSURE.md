## 1. Research Question

After candidate AX concepts are tested against verifiable actual-company cases and established DX/AX, SAP, strategy-consulting, GRC, and Decision Science domains, does a general and defensible Enterprise AX residual micro-gap remain?

## 2. Approved Closure Result

No such residual micro-gap was identified within the reviewed scope.

This is not classified as research failure. It is the appropriate termination result after candidate generation, counterexample testing, actual-case comparison, prior-domain comparison, and repeated elimination left no supported novelty claim.

The result is deliberately bounded:

> The reviewed material did not support retaining a general Enterprise AX residual micro-gap for immediate generalization or productization.

It does not establish that no gap exists anywhere.

## 3. Research Sequence Preserved

The session proceeded through the following pattern:

```text
problem definition
→ candidate generation
→ actual-company case comparison
→ primary or near-primary source review
→ established-domain comparison
→ counterexample and overlap testing
→ repeated candidate elimination
→ no retained gap within the reviewed scope
```

The source-review sequence included actual enterprise failure and transformation contexts and product, regulatory, investigative, and research materials. The referenced conversation is retained as the research trace, but it is not itself promoted into an Evidence Record and no repository source archive was created in this closure.

## 4. Evidence-Discipline Correction

The session confirmed a material methodological risk: synthetic cases can make a new concept look coherent even when the underlying enterprise recurrence and novelty have not been verified.

The clearest instance was `Claim-State Delta`. A constructed reporting chain supported explanation and hypothesis formation, but it did not establish that the proposed transformation recurs in actual enterprise reporting, is decision-material, or remains unaddressed by existing methods. The candidate was withdrawn when that boundary became explicit.

Research discipline for this track is therefore strengthened as follows:

- use synthetic cases only for explanation, test design, or falsification structure;
- do not count synthetic cases as evidence of an enterprise gap;
- require verifiable actual-company cases for recurrence and materiality claims;
- prefer primary and near-primary materials for company, product, incident, regulatory, and operating-model claims;
- compare every novelty candidate with established adjacent domains before retention; and
- preserve `no retained gap` as a valid result when the evidence does not support more.

This is a track-level research rule. It does not modify AER Core without a separate approved Decision.

## 5. Candidate Disposition

### Judgment Architecture

Not retained as a general AX gap. Relevant decision-right, challenge, escalation, governance, and operating-model functions overlap established strategy-consulting, governance, risk, assurance, and organizational-design domains.

### Semantic Integrity

Not retained as a general AX gap. Relevant data, business-semantic, reporting-fidelity, provenance, and information-quality concerns are substantially addressed by existing ERP, data-governance, reporting, and research domains. A narrower unmet combination was not established with actual recurring enterprise evidence.

### Reference Judgment

Not retained as a general AX gap. Independent challenge, reference comparison, decision governance, and assurance functions overlap GRC, audit, risk, and Decision Science. The session did not establish a distinct general residual object.

### Claim-State Delta

Withdrawn. The candidate was derived too strongly from a synthetic reporting example. Its component ideas were plausible and adjacent research existed, but the session lacked actual-company reporting-chain evidence sufficient to establish recurrence, materiality, or an unmet enterprise control gap.

### Cognitive Executability

Not retained as a general residual gap. SAP and adjacent enterprise-platform evolution already address machine-executable business processes, semantics, workflow, and bounded cognitive work, while strategy consulting addresses the associated operating-model and decision-right redesign. The explanatory mechanism may remain useful, but no distinct gap claim survived.

## 6. Interpretation

The repeated elimination is the central result. The study did not fill an empty result with a renamed combination of existing disciplines and did not convert explanatory vocabulary into validated novelty.

The most fact-aligned conclusion is therefore:

> Within the current investigation boundary, there is no defensible general Enterprise AX residual micro-gap to preserve as a new concept.

This demonstrates useful stop behavior: AER can end a research path after falsification without becoming a concept factory.

## 7. Absence of Evidence and Evidence of Absence

The session found insufficient support for a retained gap. It did not prove universal nonexistence.

Accordingly:

- `not identified within scope` is permitted;
- `does not exist` is not permitted;
- future local or domain-specific gaps remain possible; and
- reopening requires new observed evidence, not dissatisfaction with the null result.

## 8. Reopening Condition

Reopen only when actual enterprise work or a material technology change reveals a concrete repeated failure that existing SAP or ERP capabilities, strategy-consulting operating-model methods, GRC or assurance controls, and Decision Science do not adequately explain or address.

The evidence must support recurrence, material consequence, and residual distinctness after competing-domain comparison. Synthetic examples, terminology changes, or a desire for a proprietary AX concept do not qualify.
